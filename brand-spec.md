# Bliss Decor — Brand Spec

## Design read
A luxury event styling and management studio for couples, hosts and brands who want celebratory spaces with a distinct point of view. The site uses editorial storytelling, tactile photography and a warm, festive palette to make the brand feel premium without becoming cold.

- **Artifact:** single-page studio landing page
- **Audience:** people planning weddings, social celebrations, launches and gala events
- **Visual language:** editorial luxury / modern Indian celebration / gallery-led storytelling
- **Mode:** greenfield
- **Visual variance:** 8/10
- **Motion intensity:** 4/10
- **Information density:** 5/10
- **Asset dependence:** 9/10
- **Brand fidelity:** 8/10 (new brand concept; official logo/assets not supplied)

## Design decisions
- **Color palette:** antique gold `#c18a36`, light gold `#e8c778`, warm ivory `#f7f0e4`, deep plum `#2b2028`, wine `#6e2a38`, coral `#be6748`
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond for editorial headlines and lockups; DM Sans for navigation, body copy and labels
- **Spacing:** 8px base unit; section padding 84–132px; large editorial gaps for calm luxury
- **Border radius:** mostly sharp image frames, rounded-pill CTAs, circular brand and icon details
- **Shadow hierarchy:** soft, low-contrast shadows only on modal, stamp and interactive elevation
- **Motion:** `cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1)` for reveal and hover; under 500ms; reduced-motion fallback included

## Assets
- `/manus-storage/...` generated imagery to be added after media generation completes
- The `BD` circular monogram in the lockup is a temporary brand concept mark, not a claimed official logo

## Reference translation
The supplied Gatherly reference informed the top navigation, dark image-led hero, serif headline treatment, modular service/event sections and clear CTA rhythm. Bliss Decor intentionally moves the genre from event discovery to luxury event studio storytelling.
